01 About

I'm a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at BRAC University, and an MSc researcher working in Human-Computer Interaction. My research sits at the intersection of games HCI, usable privacy and security, and technology design for Global South contexts; I'm aiming my work at venues like CHI and CSCW.

Before moving into HCI, I worked on reinforcement learning and applied machine learning, including an undergraduate thesis on adaptive Bitcoin blockchain networks. That background still shapes how I think about building systems around how people actually behave, rather than how a spec assumes they will.

Longer term, I'm working toward a PhD and a research career centered on games HCI.
